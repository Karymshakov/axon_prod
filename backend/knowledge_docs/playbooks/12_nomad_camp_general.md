## Nomad Camp — Quick Facts (EN)

Nomad Camp is a year-round hotel on the southern shore of Issyk-Kul lake, Kadjy-Say village, Kyrgyzstan.

Best for: families, friends, sports teams, corporate groups, and retreats.
Open 365 days a year — not just in summer.

**5 reasons guests choose Nomad Camp:**
1. Only full-scale sports infrastructure on the south shore: 25m indoor pool, gym, combat sports, padel courts.
2. Year-round — sports camps, retreats, and corporate events work in any season.
3. Space for different guest types: families, athletes, business groups coexist comfortably.
4. Live events: camps, festivals, tournaments — the hotel is always active.
5. Location + scale: south shore of Issyk-Kul with capacity for groups, forums, and teams.
